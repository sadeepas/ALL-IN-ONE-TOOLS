Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know.

Clear answers to common questions about SL Workspace tools, client-side privacy, and offline features.

Are all SL Workspace tools 100% free with zero hidden limits?
Yes. Every single tool, generator, converter, and standalone application in SL Workspace is 100% free forever. There are no paywalls, no watermarks, no daily usage limits, and no registration or account signup required.
Are my uploaded files, images, or confidential text sent to remote servers?
No! Privacy is built into our core architecture. All image resizing, PDF conversions, encryption, and audio pitch detections process 100% client-side inside your browser engine using modern HTML5 Canvas, Web Crypto API, and Web Audio APIs. Your raw files and text data never leave your local device.
How do the Pop-Up Micro-Tools work?
Simply click any tool card with the "Pop-Up Tool" badge in the main workspace grid. A sleek frosted glass modal window will launch instantly on your screen without requiring a full page reload or distracting page navigation.
How does the Workspace Backup & Restore system function?
Click the Backup button in the header or hero section to export a portable `.json` file containing your starred favorite tools, scratchpad notes, theme preference, and task lists. You can restore this file anytime on any browser or computer.
Can I use SL Workspace offline without an active internet connection?
Yes! Once loaded into your browser, SL Workspace functions completely offline since all calculation algorithms, canvas scripts, and text transformers run locally inside your browser runtime.
Can I access these tools on mobile phones, tablets, and smartboards?
Absolutely. All 50+ tools, pop-up dialogs, touch drawing canvases, and navigation bars are fully responsive and touch-optimized for iPhones, Android smartphones, iPads, and digital smartboards.
How do I launch tools quickly using the Command Palette (Ctrl + K)?
Press `Ctrl + K` (or `Cmd + K` on Mac) anywhere on the page to open the Command Palette overlay. Start typing any tool name or keyword to instantly find and launch that tool.
How does the Instrument Tuner detect musical pitch?
It requests temporary microphone permission to capture real-time audio input, performing high-speed pitch detection algorithms to display musical notes (A, B, C, D, E, F, G) and exact frequencies in Hz.
Does the Quick Scratchpad automatically save my text notes?
Yes! Anything you type into the Quick Scratchpad auto-saves in real-time to your browser's persistent `localStorage`. You can also download your notes as a `.txt` file at any time.
How can I star and pin my favorite tools to the top filter?
Click the star icon (`★`) on any tool card in the grid. Then click the "Favorites" filter tab at the top of the grid to instantly filter your pinned essential tools.
Can I convert PNG and JPG images into PDF documents for free?
Yes. Launch the PDF & Document Utility tool, drag and drop your image, and click "Convert Image to PDF" to instantly generate a printable PDF document directly within your browser.
